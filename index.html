Waypoint 01 · The problem

Large buildings still confuse the people inside them.

Hospitals, airports, university campuses and shopping centers swallow visitors in corridors that all look the same. Traditional indoor positioning depends on dense beacon grids, expensive deployments and ongoing maintenance — and even then the experience often falls short on accessibility and privacy.

01

Hardware-heavy

Beacons, anchors and wiring drive cost up and slow rollouts down.

02

Fragile UX

Drift, dead-zones and broken floor plans erode visitor trust.

03

Privacy gaps

Cloud-tracked location histories raise legitimate concerns.

04

Closed admin

Updating a route shouldn't require a vendor on-site.

Waypoint 02 · The solution

A clear path, drawn on the world itself.

Vectura AI overlays intuitive AR cues directly onto the live camera view of any modern smartphone — turning hallways, lobbies and stairwells into a guided experience. No physical infrastructure. No app fatigue. Just the next arrow, exactly where it needs to be.

Hardware-free

Vision-based localization replaces beacon grids and proprietary anchors entirely.

AR-native

Directional cues, distance hints and destination markers rendered in context.

Privacy-first

Localization runs on-device. Locations are not silently logged or sold.

Clarity engaged
Waypoint 03 · Mechanism

From floor plan to live guidance, in four steps.

Vectura AI was designed so that venue teams can take ownership of their own indoor experience — no specialized hardware vendor required.

  1. Step 01

    Map the venue

    Admins walk and capture each floor with a guided mobile flow that builds a navigable spatial graph.

  2. Step 02

    Define destinations

    Rooms, gates, counters and points of interest are tagged and arranged into routable destinations.

  3. Step 03

    Visitors scan to start

    End users open a link, point the camera at a known anchor and the route locks instantly.

  4. Step 04

    AR guides in real time

    Floating arrows, path lines and waypoint hints adapt continuously to the user's pose.
