# Marker Printing & Placement Guide

## Print Specifications
- **Size:** 21 × 21 cm (8.3 × 8.3 in)
- **Paper:** Matte (avoid glossy — causes glare)
- **Print quality:** 300 DPI minimum, high contrast
- **Source file:** `sample/demo-building/markers/entrance_marker_main.png`
- **Background:** White quiet zone (≥ 2 cm border around marker)

## Placement
- **Height:** Mount center at ~1.2m above floor (chest height)
- **Surface:** Flat wall or rigid board — no curves or wrinkles
- **Orientation:** Square, level, not rotated
- **Location:** Near the actual entrance/start point of the demo route

## Lighting
- **Good:** Diffused overhead lighting, even illumination
- **Bad:** Direct sunlight on marker, shadows across marker, backlit marker
- **Minimum:** Enough light to clearly see the marker pattern with naked eye

## Detection Tips
- **Distance:** Hold phone 0.5–2.0m from marker for best detection
- **Angle:** Face marker approximately head-on (within ~30°)
- **Hold steady:** Keep phone still for 1–2 seconds during initial scan
- **First scan:** May take 3–5 seconds; subsequent re-scans are faster

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Not detecting | Check print quality, ensure matte paper |
| Slow detection | Move closer (0.5m), improve lighting |
| Misaligned arrows | Re-print marker, ensure flat mount |
| Won't detect at all | Use Simulate Scan fallback |
